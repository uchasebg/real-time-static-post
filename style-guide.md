# Style guide: real-time static posts

## Scope and platform (confirmed by the user, 2026-09-30)

- **Platform:** the identical caption goes to Facebook and Instagram. The 2,200-character limit is Instagram's.
- **No hashtags.** None at all.
- **BG posts (Уча.се)** follow exactly the same structure and tone as IT and RO.
- **No image suggestions.** Deliver only the hook + caption unless the user asks.
- **Topics:** the user usually supplies a link or topic. Avoid politics, big tragedies and tabloid/yellow news. If a
  requested topic touches these, say so briefly before writing.
- **Two post types:** (1) **achievement / pride posts**, the default, see the structure below; (2) **memorial posts**
  on the death of a famous person, see "Memorial posts".

## Hook (on the image)

- Short, headline-like, 6–12 words. It **states** a fact that triggers pride or curiosity. It doesn't ask the reader
  anything, except in the "Кой е X…?" profile pattern.
- Ingredients: nationality + achievement + a weighty word (историческо, злато, първи, световен, завладяха).
- Patterns that work:
  - "Историческо злато за България в художествената гимнастика"
  - "4 studenti italiani hanno conquistato il mondo della cybersecurity"
  - "Първият подиум за Румъния в историята на WorldSkills"
  - Profile: "Кой е Карло Алберто Каниато – новата надежда на италианския тенис?"
- Always offer 1 main hook and 2 alternatives.

## Caption structure (in this order)

1. **Emotional opener + 1–2 emojis.** Frame the story as national pride: "Огромна победа за България! 🥇",
   "L'Italia ha dimostrato ancora una volta…", "Историческо постижение за Румъния! 🥉".
2. **Concrete facts:** who, where, what, when. Use full names, school/university, city, event name.
3. **Scale:** "Представи си мащаба:" followed by participants, countries, jury, prize.
4. **Curiosity pivot.** Each one promises another reason to keep reading:
   "И най-интересното?", "А ето и нещо, което прави успеха още по-впечатляващ:", "А ето и какво прави историята му
   още по-впечатляваща:".
5. **👉 bullet list** of achievements, prizes, or what the discipline involves. Keep bullets short, end each with ";".
   Use one list per post.
6. **Explain for a general audience:** one plain sentence that makes a technical term tangible ("С други думи – точно
   уменията, които стоят зад приложенията, които използваме всеки ден.").
7. **People behind it:** mentor, coach, team. Add a short verified quote if one exists (rector, coach, athlete).
8. **Forward teaser**, if something is coming next (next edition, next goal). Hedge it if single-sourced.
9. **Values paragraph (the brand link):** it's not luck, it's work, discipline, persistence, mentors, curiosity,
   courage. Examples: "Такива успехи не идват за една нощ.", "Зад тях стоят…".
10. **CTA (no hashtags after it):** share, or like + share, "за да научат повече хора…". An optional question comes before it ("Хареса ли ти…?").

## Memorial posts (death of a famous person)

About who the person was, their achievements and their life. The rules below are a proposed default. Refine them in
`learnings.md` after the first approved memorial.

- **Hook (user rule, 2026-10-07):** NOT about the death. It names what the person did / is remembered for. Patterns:
  "Българинът, зад най-запомнящия се образ във „Време разделно“", "Българската актриса, която [постижение]". Never
  "Почина…" / "Сбогом…". No exclamation marks and no clickbait. Keep it ≤ 8 words; "От X до Y: …" with names known
  from school works well (approved: "От Талев до Шекспир: 30 години на една сцена").
- **Hook sub-line (user rule, 2026-10-12):** add a second line under the memorial hook: "Прочети историята на [Име] в
  описанието" / IT "Leggi la storia di [Nome] nella descrizione" / RO "Citește povestea lui/ei [Nume] în descriere".
- **Closing line per market:** BG "Поклон пред светлата му/ѝ памет. 🕯️"; IT "Onore alla sua memoria. 🕯️" (user's choice, 2026-10-12; never "Che la terra ti sia lieve").
- **Quotes:** record where each quote was first published (e.g. a relative's Facebook post) and which outlets quote it verbatim.
- **Reference memorial (user's own, Йосиф Сърчаджиев):** opens "🕊️ На 81 години си отиде [Име] – [кой беше].", then
  birth/family → education → career path with numbers → signature roles + awards with years → a quote of theirs →
  "Поклон пред светлата му/ѝ памет. 🕯️". No share CTA.
- **Caption structure:**
  1. The news in one or two sober sentences: who passed away and at what age. Give the date and place only if confirmed.
  2. Who they were: origin, education, the road to their field.
  3. Main achievements: 👉 list of works, titles, records, discoveries, awards.
  4. A human detail or a verified quote of theirs that shows character.
  5. Legacy: what they leave to the next generations and why it matters (the link to learning and inspiration).
  6. Closing line + CTA: "Сподели поста, за да си спомним заедно за [Име]." / IT "Condividi il post per ricordarlo
     insieme." / RO "Distribuie postarea, ca să ni-l amintim împreună." Don't ask for likes.
- **Tone:** respectful, warm, factual. No pivots like "И най-интересното?", and no celebratory emojis (🥇🚀👏). Use at most 1–2
  quiet emojis (🕊️ or 🖤), or none.
- **Cause of death:** mention it only if the family or an official source confirmed it, and never speculate. Leave out
  details about illness or private life unless they are central and public.
- Fact-checking is as strict as for other posts. Obituaries often repeat errors (birth dates, number of titles), so
  cross-check every number.

## Tone and form

- Informal "ти" (IT "tu", RO "tu"). Warm, proud, energetic, but factual. No clickbait lies, no exaggeration
  beyond what the sources say.
- Short paragraphs of 1–4 sentences with blank lines between them. Mobile-first.
- **3–6 emojis in total**, used as accents: 🎾🏆🥇🥉🇮🇹🇷🇴🧠🚀💻👏. Plus 👉 for bullets.
- Numbers as digits. Scores: BG "7:6, 6:4", IT "7-6 6-4".
- **Length: ≤ 2,200 characters per caption** (UTF-16, emoji = 2). Target 1,700–2,000. Trim in this order: long
  explanations inside parentheses, secondary biography details, merged idol/role-model sentences. Keep the core
  structure.

## Accuracy language (hedges)

Use these when a fact is single-sourced or interpretive:
- BG: "според медиите", "според специализираните медии", "смята се", "описва се като", "по думите му", "самият той споделя".
- IT: "secondo la stampa specializzata", "viene descritto come", "secondo quanto riportato", "racconta di".
- RO: "potrivit presei", "este descris ca", "după cum a declarat", "se pare că" (use sparingly).

## Translation standard (IT for eduboom.it, RO for eduboom.ro)

- Write it as a native writer for an educational brand would: natural, polished, standard register. No slang, no
  Bulgarian calques, no word-for-word structure. Rebuild sentences the way that country's press writes them.
- Keep the same facts, hedges, structure, bullets and emojis. The hook may be adapted, not literally translated.
- Use the local names and official titles exactly as the official source writes them. Quotes stay in the original
  language, taken verbatim from the source.
- The CTA matches the market's reference post. IT: "Metti like e condividi il post, così … arriverà a più persone!". RO:
  "Distribuie postarea, ca să afle cât mai mulți oameni despre…".
- Before saving, do a native-speaker self-review sentence by sentence: tense and agreement, repetitions, register,
  and natural collocations.
